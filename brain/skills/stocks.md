# Stock Trading Skill

## Philosophy

Swing trading with a 2–8 week holding horizon. You are not a day trader.
The goal is to capture directional moves with favorable risk/reward and cut losses fast.
Patience between setups is part of the strategy — frequency is not.

## Entry Criteria

Require at least 2 of the following before opening any new position:

**Trend**
- Price is above its 20-day moving average and the MA itself is sloping upward
- Stock is making higher highs and higher lows over the last 10 sessions

**Momentum**
- Price has moved at least 3% in your favor over the last 5 sessions with no reversal candle
- RSI (14-period) is between 50–70: trending up but not yet overbought

**Volume**
- Recent session volume is at least 20% above the 30-day average (confirms conviction)
- Volume expanded on the up days and contracted on the down days

**Catalyst or Sector Tailwind**
- Earnings beat with raised guidance (within last 5 sessions)
- Analyst upgrade or price target raise
- Sector is in rotation: broad money is moving into this sector right now
- Congressional or institutional mirror trade aligned with this ticker (check alerts)

**Pullback Setup**
- Stock pulled back 5–15% from a recent high on low volume, into a known support level
- Broader market trend is still intact — you are buying a dip, not catching a falling knife

## Exit Criteria

Exit immediately (same cycle) if:
- Position is down more than 8% from your average cost — do not hold, do not hope
- The original thesis is broken (earnings miss, guidance cut, key executive departure)

Exit at target if:
- Position is up 20–30%: take at least half off the table, let the rest run with a tighter stop
- Position is up more than 40%: exit fully and look for the next setup

Exit on deterioration if:
- Price crosses below the 20-day MA on above-average volume
- Market conditions shift to a confirmed downtrend (broad indices below 50-day MA)
- A better opportunity exists and capital is tied up in a stalled position

## Position Sizing

- Open new positions at 5–10% of total portfolio value — never go full size immediately
- Add to a winning position only after it confirms: up >5% from entry and trend intact
- Maximum single position: 20% of portfolio (guardrail enforced — do not attempt to exceed)
- Maintain a minimum 15% cash reserve at all times for new opportunities

## Sector and Stock Filters

Prefer:
- Large-cap and mid-cap stocks with market cap > $2B (liquidity, analyst coverage)
- Stocks in sectors with current macro tailwinds (check recent alerts and watchlist activity)
- Companies with positive earnings growth trend over the last 2 quarters

Avoid:
- Stocks under $5 (penny stocks: manipulation risk, wide spreads)
- Stocks with earnings announcements within 5 days — IV spike risk, unpredictable gap
- Highly shorted stocks with short interest > 20% of float unless you have a squeeze thesis
- Companies with significant debt refinancing risk in a rising rate environment

## Watchlist Usage

Your watchlist represents tickers the user wants monitored, not automatic buy signals.
Evaluate each watchlist ticker each cycle. Articulate why you are acting or not acting.
If a ticker has been on the watchlist for 3+ cycles with no setup, note it in an alert.

## Market Conditions Override

Do not open new stock positions if:
- The S&P 500 is in a confirmed downtrend (below its 50-day MA and falling)
- The VIX is above 30 (high fear — wait for stabilization)
- The portfolio has already hit the daily loss halt guardrail

In these conditions: hold cash, protect existing positions, and report in an alert.
